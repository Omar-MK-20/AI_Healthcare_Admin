CITY GENERAL HOSPITAL - ADMINISTRATION POLICY MANUAL
Version 2.1 - Effective 2026

1. APPOINTMENT SCHEDULING RULES
- General Checkup: Monday to Friday only, 09:00 to 16:00. Must be booked at least 1 day in advance.
- Specialist Visit (Cardiology, Dermatology, Orthopedics): Monday to Thursday only, 10:00 to 15:00. Requires a referral note. Valid departments: Cardiology, Dermatology, Orthopedics, General.
- Lab Test: Monday to Saturday, 08:00 to 18:00. No referral needed.
- Emergency cases are NOT booked by this system. Patients with chest pain, severe bleeding, or breathing difficulty must call emergency or visit ER immediately.
- No appointments on Sundays. No appointments in the past.
- Double-booking the same doctor at the same date and time is strictly forbidden.

3. INSURANCE RULES
- Insurance types accepted: Basic, Premium, None.
- Basic covers: General Checkup and Lab Test only. It does NOT cover Specialist visits.
- Premium covers: General, Specialist, and Lab Test.
- Patients with 'None' must pay in full before appointment confirmation.
- Insurance ID format: INS-XXXX (example INS-1001). If ID is missing or invalid, treat as 'None'.

4. BILLING RULES
- General Checkup: 300 EGP
- Specialist Visit: 800 EGP
- Lab Test: 450 EGP
- Premium insurance gives 20% discount on Specialist visits only.
- Basic insurance covers General and Lab in full (patient pays 0).

5. MEDICAL REPORT SUMMARIZATION
- Summaries must be short (3-5 bullet points), plain language, no diagnosis invention.
- Never prescribe medication or dosage. If report mentions medication, copy it exactly without changing dose.
- Always add disclaimer: 'This is an administrative summary, not medical advice.'

6. PRIVACY AND GUARDRAILS
- Never output full National ID. Mask it (e.g. show last 4 digits only).
- Never provide diagnosis or treatment recommendations.
- If user asks for diagnosis, dosage, or emergency help, refuse and redirect to a doctor / ER.
- All automated emails must include appointment date, time, doctor, department, and price.
